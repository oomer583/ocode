# Contributing

OCode is developed by one person. It is not an open contribution project.

## Pull requests

Not accepted. This repository exists so the work is recorded and backed up, not
to collect patches. Pull requests will be closed without review.

## Bugs and ideas

These are welcome, and the place for them is the community:

https://discord.gg/QuV6chaxUq

Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. For a crash, the
app version and your operating system help.

## Upstream

Most of the code here comes from [opencode](https://github.com/sst/opencode) and
is MIT licensed. If your change belongs there rather than in OCode, send it to
them instead. It will reach far more people.

## Name and logo

The OCode name and the OCode logo are not part of the MIT grant and are not
